## About Me

I'm a **Flutter and Dart developer** with **3 years of experience** building **cross-platform mobile apps** for **Android and iOS**. I currently work at **Software Ignit**, where I build production apps with clean structure, REST API connections, and Firebase backends.

I care about three things: **apps that look good**, **code that is easy to grow later**, and **apps that work in real life**, even when the internet is slow or off.

I'm based in Swat, Pakistan, and I'm happy to work with clients and teams anywhere in the world.

## How I Can Help

- **Build your mobile app from scratch.** One Flutter codebase gives you both Android and iOS, so you reach more customers with less cost.

- **Turn your design into a real app.** I convert Figma designs into pixel-perfect Flutter screens.
